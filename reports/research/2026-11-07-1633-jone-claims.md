## Claims Jone should not route as testable today

- **"Player X is a better shooter than average."** `kernels.beta_binomial_posterior` gives a posterior, which is
  descriptive. There is no registered decision rule, so the Referee should return `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`/`BLOCKED`.
- **Anything about markets beating or losing to the model, profit or edge.** Out of scope (CLAUDE.md §2). The
  forecast/quote comparison is descriptive only, and no live or recorded market data have been run.
- **Causal claims** ("the hot hand is real", "rest causes wins"). The registered tests detect association
  under a stated null only.
- **Any claim that needs the 2023–24 final games**, until Max decides on a new epoch.
