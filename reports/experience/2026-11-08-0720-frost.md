## What was built

- **Sequence:** Heater's take → Frost's question → its result chip → (repeat, at most 3 times) → Ref card → stamp → Frost's reaction → Heater's reaction. This is `exchangeSteps` in `web/frost/frost-model.js`. `runExchange` in `demo.js` plays the steps in that order.
- **Layout:** while Frost is on stage, `body[data-cast="frost"]` sets:
  - Heater on the left and Frost on the right (columns 28/44/28fr);
  - the conversation in the middle.

  Frost slides in from the right on his first question. The Ref card and the stamp are centred under the conversation.
- **Bubbles:** they alternate:
  - Heater's are orange, with the tail on the left;
  - Frost's are ice-blue (`--ice: #8FD3FF`), with the tail on the right.

  There are no name or type tags: a test checks that each bubble is one `<li>` with one `<p>`. Each result chip is a small chip under its Frost question, classed pass, fail or info. The chip text always says the result in words.
- **Voices:** there are three:
  - Frost speaks en-GB at rate 0.95, pitch 1.0, with a British voice that is neither Heater's nor the Ref's.
  - If Frost takes the Ref's only British voice, the Ref moves to another English voice.
  - `pickVoices` and `VOICE` are unchanged. `heater.js` gets an optional `voicePicker`, which is set only while Frost is on stage.
- **Space:** Space skips to the stamp. Every step before the stamp is drawn at once with no speech; the stamp and both reactions still play. Space is ignored:
  - in text fields other than the question box;
  - with modifier keys held;
  - on key repeat;
  - when no exchange is running.

  A hint, "Press Space to skip to the verdict", is shown while an exchange runs.
- **Narrow screens (≤860px):** small 160px portraits of Heater and Frost run along the top, with the conversation below.
- **Fallback:** when the answer has no Frost block, or the take was forced, the Heater-only flow runs exactly as before.
- **Missing poses:** a missing pose is labelled on stage as "pose image missing: frost/poses/X.webp". No other image is used in its place.
