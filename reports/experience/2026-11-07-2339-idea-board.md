## What it does

1. **`GET /api/ideas`** (`apps/web/server/ideas.py`, read-only). It reads Research's idea-family
   directory: `--ideas`, default `handoff/research-20261107/evidence/idea-family`.
   - A family is shown only when all of these hold:
     - `results.registration_hash` equals the sha256 of `registration.json`;
     - the results list exactly the registered ideas, and `n_ideas` agrees;
     - verdicts are only `CANDIDATE`, `NOT_A_CANDIDATE` or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`;
     - for a synthetic family, `truth.json` (`revealed_after: results.json`) and `scoreboard.json` both
       cite the sha256 of `results.json`;
     - the JSON is strict (no NaN, no duplicate keys).
   - Anything else is listed under "Not shown" with its reason.
   - Without the directory, the route returns 404 with this reason: "no idea-family results in this
     checkout (Research's research/idea-family is not merged here)".
2. **"Idea board" button** (`web/ideas/idea-board.js`, `idea-model.js`). Also opens with `demo.html?ideas=1`.
   - **Development family.** Research's 12 ideas on 2023-24 development games, headed
     "12 ideas tested together on 2023-24 development games: 4 worth a proper test". Each card shows:
     - the idea as a sentence, e.g. "Home teams win more when the home team is the Elo favourite by 100+
       points";
     - Research's numbers: "Home teams won 68.3% of 460 such games, 42.5% of the other 466";
     - the chance check: "p = 0.012 after allowing for the 12 ideas tested together (before: 0.001)";
     - the verdict: Worth a proper test / No sign of it / Too little data.
   - A CANDIDATE always carries this line: "A lead, not a finding: it needs its own pre-registered test
     on games nobody has looked at."
   - **"Ask Heater about this idea"** plays the sequence:
     - Heater pitches the idea, with no digits.
     - The Ref words Research's verdict and numbers.
     - The stamp lands: WORTH A TEST, NO SIGN or TOO LITTLE DATA.
     - Heater ends in thinking (candidate), cooled or unsure. Never vindicated and never SURVIVED: an idea
       is exploratory.
     - "Show the evidence" lists: the idea hash, the raw and Holm p, B, the registration time and hash
       (before any result), the results sha256, and the checks made here.
   - **Synthetic leagues**, labelled SYNTHETIC:
     - γ = 0.30: "Found 1 of 9 planted effects; missed 8. Correctly ignored 4 of 4 ideas with nothing
       there. False alarms: 0."
     - γ = 0 (control): found 1 of 1, ignored 12 of 12, 0 false alarms.
     - The board notes that each is one league, so these are counts, not rates.
3. Every number is read from Research's files. The page computes no statistic or verdict, and
   calls only `/api/ideas` (relative).

**Fixture.** Byte copies of `research/idea-family@a84bd64`'s `dev_example`, `synthetic_gamma0` and
`synthetic_gamma030` files are in `apps/web/tests/fixtures/idea_family/`. Each copy's git blob equals the
source blob; `PROVENANCE.md` lists the sha256s. The demo page never reads this copy.

**`apps/web/tests/test_ideas.py`** (11 tests) checks:
- all three families verify, and the reader never writes;
- the committed `/api/ideas` capture equals the reader output;
- these are refused:
  - a changed registration;
  - a dropped idea;
  - a scoreboard or truth that doesn't cite these results;
  - a scoreboard without a truth file;
  - a "SURVIVED_PROTOCOL" verdict;
  - NaN, or a duplicate key;
- a missing directory returns 404 with the reason;
- the route works.

**`apps/web/tests/ideas.test.mjs`** (8 tests) runs against the server's own `/api/ideas` output. It checks:
- only relative URLs are used;
- the plain sentences for every idea;
- the exact numbers and chance-check wording;
- no CANDIDATE is worded or posed as a finding;
- Heater's lines have no digits;
- the synthetic tallies;
- rendering: 12 idea cards with Ask buttons, 2 SYNTHETIC cards without;
- the "no results" and "not shown" text.
