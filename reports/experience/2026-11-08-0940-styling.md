## Changes

1. **Result chips read as facts, not a speaker.**
   - Centred in the thread.
   - Grey `#9B97AD` text on a subtle dark background (`rgba(255,255,255,.045)`).
   - No tail; 16 px against 18 px bubbles.
   - The ice-blue left border is the only colour: the pass/fail green and red borders are gone.
2. **Much larger Heater and Frost.** Both figures run the stage height beside the conversation. The
   ask box now sits under the middle column only.

   | Screen | Middle column | Figure height |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1920x1080 | 640 px | 88% of the viewport (950 px) |
   | 1440x900 | 560 px | as large as the 392 px side columns allow (about 72%) |

   - Sizing: `--fig-h` is the smallest of 88vh, the space above the footer under the label, and
     165% of the column width (each side column is a size container).
   - **Clipping:** I measured every pose. Heater's drawn figure spans 8–93% of the image width,
     Frost's 30–70%. So the image box may be wider than its column; only its empty edges are clipped
     (`overflow: clip`).
   - **Feet:** the figures stand just above the footer (`position: sticky`) while a long
     conversation scrolls. Frost's box sits 3.5% lower so both characters' feet line up.
   - **Heater only:** the size change applies only with Frost on stage. Heater alone on the home view
     is unchanged.
3. **Name labels.**
   - **HEATER:** Oswald 700, uppercase, 4 px letter-spacing, `#FF7A1A`.
   - **FROST:** Nunito 700, `#8FD3FF`.
   - **Both:** 44 px with a faint glow in their colour, centred over the figure, overlapping the empty
     top of the image. Shown only with both characters on stage; 22 px on phones.
   - Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts in `demo.html`.
   - **CSP:** the demo server's Content-Security-Policy (`apps/web/server/app.py`) blocked outside
     styles and fonts, so I allowed exactly `https://fonts.googleapis.com` (styles) and
     `https://fonts.gstatic.com` (fonts). `script-src` and `connect-src` stay `'self'`, and a test
     pins this.
   - **Privacy:** the demo page now contacts Google when it loads. If the fonts don't load (offline),
     the labels fall back to Bebas Neue / Quicksand if installed, else the page's condensed font /
     system-ui.
4. **The question replaces the HEATCHECK wordmark during an exchange.**
   - Style: the page's own body font (system-ui), bold, 26 px, `#ECEAF4`, centred in the space left
     of the view buttons, which stay where they were.
   - A market card's Ask shows "Walk me through this game", the message the card sends to the API.
   - The idea board, scenario cards and tamper button send no question, so they keep the wordmark.
   - The home view and the board and idea views show the wordmark.
5. **Ref card.** "THE REF" is double size (1.56rem), with no claim title beside it.
   - **First line, the headline:** 30 px bold capitals in `#2EC4B6`. It uses Max's `ref_headline` when
     the answer has one.
   - **Fallback headline (provisional):** Max's field and patterns are not on any pushed branch yet,
     so until then the page builds `<stamp words> — <claim title>`, e.g. "NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE — HOT
     HAND: THREE MAKES IN A ROW (FINAL TEST)". It's one function, `refHeadline` in
     `web/frost/frost-model.js`, so it can be changed to Max's exact wording.
   - **Second line:** `ref_text` at 22 px and 70% opacity. It's still the full multi-sentence text
     until Max shortens it.
   - **Other flows:** tamper, idea and what-if cards show their old title as the headline.
