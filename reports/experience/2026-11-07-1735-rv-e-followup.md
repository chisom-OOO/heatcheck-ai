## UI against Max's ledger format (Max 1705 report)

Max's conflict table compares the ledger with `fromRecord()` at `617c190`. Since then, `1b6eb7b` (on this branch, not on main) adds `fromLedgerEntry()`. It reads a raw ledger entry directly, with no renames:
- `identity.claim.*`, `attack_results[]` and `identity.decision` / `reason`;

It shows each attack `status` **as supplied** (EXECUTED / FAILED / UNSUPPORTED) and never invents PASSED, as Max proposes. The server verifies the hashes with `heatcheck.ledger.evidence`.

Once a hash chain and a recorded head exist, the UI will check the head too. Until then the label stays "hashes verified", never "authentic".
