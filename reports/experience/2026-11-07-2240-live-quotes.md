## What was built: `heatcheck/markets/live_quotes.py`

**`validate`** checks every rule of the shared `heatcheck-live-forecast/1` format:
- `format`;
- probabilities in [0, 1], refusing NaN and booleans;
- UTC timestamps ending in `Z`;
- `data_cutoff_utc` before every `tip_utc`;
- `nba:<10 digits>` game ids with a known season-type prefix, and no repeats;
- `nba-team:` ids for known teams, with home and away different;
- each team name naming only that team;
- the backtest ranges.

A file whose `source` says SAMPLE may leave `fetched_utc` and `sha256` as text. Any other file needs a real `Z` time and a 64-character hex hash.

**`capture`** captures the home team's win contract for each game, because the forecast is `p_home_win`.

1. **Derive identifiers from the game itself.** The date is the US Eastern calendar date of the tip; the DST rules are written in code, so tzdata isn't needed.
   - Polymarket slug: `nba-<away>-<home>-<date>`.
   - Kalshi event: `KXNBAGAME-<YYMONDD><AWAY><HOME>`.
   - Kalshi market: `<event>-<HOME>`.

   Nothing is searched for. A 404 is reported as NOT_FOUND, with the slug or ticker that was tried.
2. **Match exactly.** A market is MATCHED only if every check passes; the first failure makes it REJECTED, with that check as the reason.

| Venue | Checks |
|---|---|
| Polymarket | Slug equals the derived one. `sportsMarketType` is `moneyline`. Active and not closed. **`gameStartTime` equals `tip_utc` exactly.** The question names exactly the two teams. Exactly two outcomes: one names only the home team, the other only the away team. The home outcome's token is the one captured |
| Kalshi | The event ticker matches, and the event title names exactly the two teams. The market ticker is `<event>-<HOME>` with the same `event_ticker`. Binary and active. `yes_sub_title` and `title` name only the home team. `expected_expiration_time` falls after the tip and at most 6 h after it |

   **Kalshi has no tip time in the payload.** Its game identity is the date and both teams in the event ticker. A team plays at most one game a day, so that pins down the game. The expiry window is only a sanity check: a test shows that a forecast tip 30 minutes off still matches on Kalshi, while Polymarket rejects it.
3. **Book only after a match.** The book or orderbook is fetched only for a MATCHED market. A rejected market never gets its book fetched.
4. **Store and rebuild.** Every GET body is written to `raw/<sha256>.json`, with its fetch-start and received time (UTC `Z`, local clock), in `quotes.json.requests`. That includes 404s and rejected payloads. Each matched contract becomes its own `heatcheck-records/1` bundle:
   - built by the existing `capture.build` (raw bodies, a capture log, `GAME`, `VENUE_MARKET`, a `VENUE_MAPPING` marked PROPOSED, and `QUOTE`);
   - labelled `RECORDED_MARKET`;
   - re-derivable offline.

All requests go through the existing `ReadOnlyClient`: GET only, allowlisted paths, no redirects, public IPs only, a 2 MB cap. No account, key, order or authenticated endpoint is used, and no new allowlist entry was added.
