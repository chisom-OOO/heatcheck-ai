3. Run `game_prediction.py final`.

Actual: exit 0, receipt `3e238d2d…` with `receipt_was_retry: true`. H2 changed from
SURVIVED_PROTOCOL to **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** (mean 0.0341), and H1's mean became 0.0162.

recomputed. Log: `final_tampered_labels.log`.

`job_hash` covers family, epoch, plan, code and record IDs, but not data content.

reopen all 300 final games. Test: `test_rv_c1_*`.
